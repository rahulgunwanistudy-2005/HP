This report describes the reviewed demonstration corpus and the implemented source boundaries. The territory remains unverified. None of these counts establish market completeness, HP account ownership, channel authorization or bid eligibility.

## Geography, industries and entity attribution

The sample draws operating evidence from Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam and North America. Most agency and procurement discovery is Singapore-focused and English-language. It must not be described as a verified “global South East” market screen.

The screening table covers semiconductor manufacturing/equipment, precision engineering, biopharma, aerospace/MRO, logistics, financial services, professional services, hospitality, education, healthcare, retail, architecture, community care, consumer manufacturing, packaging and creative production. Breadth is purposeful; depth varies substantially.

Parent headquarters, deployment facilities and purchasing organizations are separate. Micron's opportunities are evidence-bound to Singapore. Sunway's are bound to Malaysian hospitals; Changi and THK to Singapore; LEGO to the cited Vietnam sites. ePac's multi-country capacity hypothesis and DreamWorks' broader studio relationship retain unresolved opportunity location. A global parent with a local site does not automatically make a global initiative locally eligible. Exact ePac headquarters is unresolved.

Construction contractors may buy Changi site equipment. Kuehne+Nagel operates LEGO's distribution centre and can be a distinct purchasing organization. A hospital operating subsidiary can differ from its group purchasing organization. These uncertainties remain visible instead of being merged into the parent.

## Retrieval adapters, source research and independence are different measures

The runtime has bounded HTML, JSON, feed and PDF retrieval paths. The connector registry reports whether a path is planned, tested, disabled, unavailable or manually imported; code availability alone does not prove a successful live connection. Research preparation used Firecrawl and public web retrieval, while the application runs without a Firecrawl key or paid model.

The 20 reviewed account records are curated imports. A new live run stores genuine retrieval and extraction outcomes and requires review. It is not autonomous account discovery or validated buying-intent extraction. The native runtime has no full browser-rendering or OCR fallback, no recurring source monitor, no general portal search, and no account-specific ATS feed.

Fifteen publisher-origin labels collapse into eight conservative independence groups in the current seeded model. Micron news and its filing share company origin; EDB can restate the same project. Sunway's parent and subsidiary sources share a group. Lenovo's historical and later announcements share a supplier origin. The LEGO/Kuehne+Nagel joint RDC announcement is one underlying event. Distinct publishers or URLs are not automatically independent corroboration. These counts should never be presented as the number of sources confirming a given purchase.

## Current procurement, awarded scope and stale evidence

The THK listing supplies real MFP purchasing evidence but lacks a reviewed tender pack, amendments, quantities and eligible supply route. Its stored deadline is 23 October 2026 at 12:00 Singapore time. The application can show research assessment **Active procurement** while keeping actionable readiness **Watchlist** because territory or route remains unresolved. An analyst must recheck the notice; a future demonstration must honor expiry and source freshness.

CAG notice 2026/00635 has a listed 5 November 2026 deadline but disclosed server/network/access-control scope. It is an HP Inc. product-scope exclusion. No endpoint bundle is inferred.

ePac's announced HP agreement is committed scope. Incremental capacity requires separate confirmation and must not be summed with the existing agreement. The official narrative's 10-versus-more-than-10 press wording is exposed.

TMC Life's screened MFP tender closed on 18 September 2026. Its stale “OPEN” page label does not override the past deadline. LEGO's 2025 openings and DreamWorks' historical relationship sources are retained because they improve downgrade and barrier reasoning; new retrieval timestamps do not renew the underlying events.

## Failures, partial access and evidence retention

One initial Firecrawl account search succeeded; six subsequent account searches returned “Transport closed.” Reviewed evidence continued through public web retrieval. The official product research separately records its actual tools and outcomes. This does not establish a runtime Firecrawl integration.

The EDB semiconductor overview and CAG tunnel-award announcement had limited direct extraction; their retained statements are limited to reviewed indexed excerpts. THK's annual-report link redirected to an HTML report with no extracted lines; no report claims were retained. CP ALL and AECOM source failures remain unavailable screening rows. OCBC/UOB report-access failures were recorded but excluded from the 24-account count.

Only short passages and selected metadata/fingerprints are retained. This is not a complete licensed archive of remote pages. Rechecks can require review when content changes or a retained passage disappears. Source-access restrictions were not bypassed.

## Recommendation and validation limits

The corpus has 36 qualification actions and 30 buyer-role hypotheses, with no named individual buyers. Device quantities, current installed fleets, transaction prices, margins, budget authority, current contract expiry and win probabilities are not fabricated. Organizational scale and project capex do not fill those gaps.

The manual four-case news-only comparison observed two changed pursuit/scope conclusions, one added access action and one unchanged watchlist decision. It is a selected qualitative review, not a predictive benchmark or accuracy percentage. Deterministic integrity checks verify relationships and rules, not the truth of every source or sales outcomes.
