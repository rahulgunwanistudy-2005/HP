# Panel demonstration script

A 6½-minute walkthrough of the implemented local HP Account Intelligence application. The timed sections contain about 640 spoken words, leaving room for the on-screen steps. The evidence corpus was reviewed on 16 October 2026. Use the current server address from the README.

## Before presenting

- Start the application with its reviewed dataset and open **Opportunities**. Use a dedicated demonstration database if you need repeatable notes; preserve the working database and its analyst changes.
- Check the displayed territory and source dates. The country list remains unverified unless the user has supplied and confirmed it. Do not mark a country list verified merely to make the demonstration rank more attractively.
- Inspect a real completed or partial research run in **Research operations**, or prepare one public URL for a bounded recheck. Network failure is a legitimate result; do not replace it with a fabricated success.
- Recheck the THK notice before describing its status. Its retained listing closes on 23 October 2026 at 12:00 Singapore time. After that deadline, explain expiry instead of calling it open. Full tender specifications, amendments and bid eligibility were not reviewed in the demonstration research.
- Keep this script and the source-coverage report available. Browser and software test results belong in the validation report; do not describe a check as passed merely because it appears in this script.

## 0:00–0:35 — Establish the decision and the boundary

**Open:** Opportunities, then briefly Territory & coverage.

**Say:** “This workspace helps an HP enterprise sales manager decide what to investigate selling, why, through which purchasing organization, and with which next action. Every recommendation follows account, business event, potential requirement, HP family and qualification action.”

Point to the territory banner.

**Say:** “The assignment's phrase ‘global South East’ has not yet been resolved into countries. These are authentic research demonstrations, not territory-qualified prospects. Country configuration changes eligibility and ranking without rewriting the underlying source facts.”

Point to coverage: 24 screened accounts, seven deep accounts and nine opportunities or exclusions.

## 0:35–2:00 — Inspect a genuine buying signal without overstating pursuit eligibility

**Open:** Opportunities. Search “Thye Hua Kwan” and open **Verify eligibility for the five-year MFP equipment and service tender**.

Point to the reasoning chain, hypothesis notice and score panel.

**Say:** “THK is the procurement-led case. A public buyer listing specifies multifunction printer equipment and maintenance for five years. That is explicit purchasing evidence. It does not establish HP's right to bid, device quantities, a budget or a likely win.”

Point to **Independent research assessment** and **Readiness gates**.

**Say:** “The research assessment can say Active procurement because an open buying notice was observed. The actionable record stays Watchlist while territory and supply eligibility are unresolved. The numerical score cannot override those gates.”

Open an **Evidence** link. Show the original URL, retained passage, notice identifier, publication/event/retrieval dates and limitations. Explain that the public listing was accessible, while the detailed pack and amendments remain unreviewed. If the notice has expired, show the resulting downgrade and explain why a new fetch must not reset the deadline.

Open **Buyer map**.

**Say:** “The map identifies procurement, IT/security, service operations and approval roles. It deliberately supplies no invented names or purchasing authority. The route is conditional on an eligible direct seller or authorized service partner.”

Return to **Opportunity & strategy** and expand the first action.

**Say:** “The short notice window replaces the generic 90-day sequence. First verify the live notice, mandatory conditions and authorized route. Then assess requirements. Stop if mandatory conditions or product fit fail.”

## 2:00–2:50 — Demonstrate one account with separate opportunities and a shared event

**Open:** Accounts → Micron Technology.

Show its workstation and Poly collaboration hypotheses. Open the Relationships tab and its stored relationship explorer if time permits.

**Say:** “Micron's Singapore expansion supports two different questions: engineering compute and collaboration rooms. They have separate requirements, buyer roles and qualification actions. Both refer to the same expansion event, so the application does not turn two opportunities into two independent triggers.”

Open the workstation opportunity and its October 2026 SEC filing evidence.

**Say:** “The later filing reiterates the Singapore programme, including the 2027 HBM and H2 2028 fab horizon. A later disclosure can update our understanding; it still does not establish an endpoint order. Capital spending and jobs are not converted into device quantities or IT budget.”

## 2:50–3:50 — Show why the system sometimes recommends restraint

Use the opportunity search to inspect these four cases briefly:

| Case | Point to make |
|---|---|
| ePac | “HP's signed industrial-print agreement is committed scope. We investigate only a separately verified incremental gap and avoid counting already sold equipment as new pipeline.” |
| DreamWorks | “The documented Lenovo preferred-provider relationship is a significant replacement barrier. It does not prove exclusivity or a renewal date. This stays a watchlist until an evaluation opening is verified.” |
| LEGO | “The factory and distribution centre opened in 2025. Their old opening stories do not establish a current device purchase. The logistics operator may be a separate purchasing organization.” |
| Changi AFISMS exclusion | “This current procurement title concerns servers, network and access control. A live tender is insufficient when HP Inc. product fit fails. We do not recast HPE infrastructure as an HP Inc. opportunity.” |

The aim is to show different decisions, not to read every account.

## 3:50–4:40 — Inspect real research operations

**Open:** Research operations. Select a known account.

Show an existing actual run's requested, retrieved, skipped and failed URLs, source errors and manual-review state. Alternatively, start one bounded recheck using an existing public source URL and show the genuine state transitions.

**Say:** “The reviewed seed and this live document recheck are different. The application runs without a paid service or model key. Its native retrieval extracts public documents and stores outcomes; it does not silently invent verified claims, buyer intent or opportunities from arbitrary text.”

Show the connector registry.

**Say:** “HTML, JSON, feed and PDF adapters have separate status. The reviewed import is manual research. Automatic country procurement discovery and careers discovery are not established just because an adapter exists. If this recheck fails or is partial, that outcome remains visible.”

If the run remains in progress, leave it running or cancel it through the actual control. Do not wait through the remaining presentation or claim completion prematurely.

## 4:40–5:35 — Record a truthful finding and export a useful brief

**Open:** THK opportunity → Qualification log.

Choose **Qualifying** and enter:

> Panel demonstration: public notice reviewed; detailed specifications, amendments, territory and supply eligibility still require verification. No buyer contact or bid submission has occurred.

Select **Save qualification**, reload and reopen the record to demonstrate persistence. Use this explicitly labeled demonstration note; do not claim to have verified a buyer or contacted procurement. If showing action progress, select **In progress** for notice review, not Done for unperformed work.

Open the account and select **Markdown brief** or **Print brief**.

**Say:** “The brief carries the opportunity hypothesis, sources and dates, buyer roles, barriers, unknowns and next actions. JSON and CSV are also available. This supports an account conversation and review; it does not create a monetary pipeline.”

## 5:35–6:30 — Defend the ranking and the evidence limits

**Open:** Scoring policy, then Methodology.

Point to the six weights: evidence 25, fit 20, timing 20, scale 15, access 10 and feasibility 10.

**Say:** “These are provisional policy choices. Each input is 0–4 or unknown. Unknown earns no points and the weights are not renormalized. The score is a qualification priority within a readiness tier, not purchase probability, win probability or expected revenue.”

Show the sensitivity results that the application actually computes; state whether the displayed leader changes instead of predicting the result.

**Say:** “The corpus has 20 logical account evidence records across 19 URLs and nine account source families. Fifteen publisher-origin labels are conservatively grouped into eight independence groups. There are also nine HP product families with 14 official product sources. These counts describe this reviewed sample, not complete market coverage.”

**Close:** “The defensible result is the next evidence-based decision: pursue qualification, resolve a buyer or route, nurture, or stop. The unresolved territory, tender pack, buyer authority and installed-base questions remain visible.”

## Likely panel questions

| Question | Defensible answer |
|---|---|
| Why these accounts? | A documented purposeful screen across source families, industries and buying situations. Retained examples test expansion, explicit procurement, specialized fit, barriers and staleness. It is not an exhaustive territory search. |
| Where is the AI? | The no-key version uses reviewed records and deterministic retrieval/scoring. An optional provider interface exists; no paid model or live autonomous AI research is required or claimed. |
| Why is a charity included? | THK is a procurement-led exception: a concrete five-year print scope offers stronger buying evidence than a famous company with no disclosed intent. Quantity and value remain unknown. |
| Why are high-fit accounts on Watchlist? | Territory, evidence freshness, procurement status and supply gates precede numerical fit. Research maturity and authorized pursuit readiness are different assessments. |
| How do you know these buyers? | These are relevant roles to verify. No individual or budget authority is claimed without supporting public evidence. |
| What did additional sources change? | On the four-case manual comparison, ePac and Changi changed pursuit/scope conclusions, Micron added an access action, and DreamWorks remained a watchlist. This is a small qualitative sample, not a predictive-accuracy claim. |
| Can deals close in 90 days? | The plans qualify and advance opportunities. They do not promise enterprise closes within 90 days. |
