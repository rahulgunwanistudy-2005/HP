## Discovery process and selection

1. Screen investment-agency project overviews across semiconductor, biopharma and aerospace sectors.
2. Follow original company announcements and resolve operating site, parent and project separately.
3. Add public reporting and company/institutional profiles to cover financial services, professional services, hospitality, education, retail and architecture.
4. Search public procurement for concrete buying requirements and exclusions; inspect original notices instead of relying on aggregators.
5. Retain cases that demonstrate different reasoning situations. Keep unavailable sources as failures and expired procurement as downgraded.

This is a purposeful research sample, heavily biased toward English-language Singapore sources, plus Malaysia, Vietnam and North American demonstrations. It is not an issuer census, complete country screen or statistically representative market sample. US/Canada operating evidence for ePac does not make its global opportunity country-bound. ## Independence, event resolution and contradictions

- Micron's two expansion opportunities share `micron-singapore-fab-2026`. The October 2026 SEC filing updates that same company project; it is not independent purchase-intent corroboration. EDB also restates this underlying project and provides discovery context; it does not increase intent confidence. The 2025 HBM source distinguishes initial operations from meaningful capacity. The later 2026 update controls the supply milestone.
- The Sunway prospectus, parent investor presentation and facility directory share a corporate group. The investor timing caveat qualifies the proposal; an unchanged retrieval date does not refresh the original event.
- The ePac agreement and geographic expansion are related but distinct events. HP's supplier narrative is a source with commercial incentives. Its press-count ambiguity is exposed rather than silently reconciled.
- Changi civil awards concern already allocated construction scope. Contractor equipment purchasing cannot be assigned automatically to CAG. The current AFISMS listing is an explicit scope exclusion.
- The Lenovo 2021 and 2025 relationship announcements share the same supplier origin. The newer relationship expansion does not prove exclusivity, but materially weakens immediate replacement feasibility.
- LEGO and Kuehne+Nagel's joint RDC announcement is one shared announcement, even if hosted by both parties. A separate Kuehne+Nagel screening row does not create a second underlying event.
- THK and CAG notices share a retrieval page but have separate notice identifiers, buyers, scopes and deadlines. A portal page change must trigger notice-specific re-review.

This is a small, selected qualitative comparison; no accuracy percentage, conversion or revenue impact is claimed.

Observed on these four cases: **two** had a materially different pursuit/scope conclusion (ePac, Changi); **one** added a concrete access qualification action (Micron); **one** remained unchanged (DreamWorks). These are manually reviewed comparison outcomes, not a generalized benchmark.

THK was discovered through procurement rather than included to improve the news comparison. Sunway shows the value of a prospectus and investor timing caveat, but lacks a comparable retained newsroom-only set and is therefore excluded from the four-case tally.

The first Firecrawl search (Micron Singapore facility) succeeded. The next six independent Firecrawl searches returned `Transport closed`; all are preserved in `coverage.sourceFailures`. No source-access restrictions were bypassed. No contact databases, guessed emails, private contacts, paid subscriptions, CRM fields, fleet quantities, transaction prices, margins, budgets, contract expiry dates or win probabilities were fabricated.

Remaining work requires the exact territory; site/legal-entity checks; current implementation updates; full tender-pack/amendment checks; approved buyer and route discovery; installed-base and contract evidence; and customer validation of each requirement. The ordinal score inputs are provisional analyst judgments, with missing critical facts left null. Readiness gates must override numerical fit or timing.
